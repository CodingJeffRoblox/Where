# Code of conduct

Where should be a friendly project to take part in, whoever you are.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and kind.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Accept that decisions sometimes go the other way.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately
through [GitHub](https://github.com/ItsJeffTheDev). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock discussions, or
block people who break these rules.

This code is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
